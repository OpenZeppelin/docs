This module provides the full ConfidentialFungibleToken module API.

</Callout>

The page covers the following modules:

- [ConfidentialFungibleToken](#ConfidentialFungibleToken): the token with ElGamal-encrypted balances.
- [ConfidentialFungibleTokenPublicSupply](#ConfidentialFungibleTokenPublicSupply): an extension that tracks a public total supply.

#### EscrowEntry [toc] [#ConfidentialFungibleToken-EscrowEntry]
#### [!toc] [#ConfidentialFungibleToken-EscrowEntry]
<APIItemCompact
  circuitSig='struct EscrowEntry { spenderCt: ElGamal_Ciphertext; ownerCt: ElGamal_Ciphertext; ownerMemo: EcdhMask_Ciphertext; }'
  kind="struct"
  id="ConfidentialFungibleToken-EscrowEntry"
>
  The encrypted allowance an owner granted a spender. `spenderCt` and `ownerCt` are two
  [ElGamal ciphertexts](./crypto#ElGamal-Ciphertext) of the same remaining amount, one under each party's key.
  `ownerMemo` is an [EcdhMask ciphertext](./crypto#EcdhMask-Ciphertext) that lets the owner read the current
  remaining allowance directly with its encryption secret; it is set to the cap on `approve` and refreshed to the
  reduced remainder on every spend. There is no public cap.
</APIItemCompact>

#### wit_PlaintextBalance [toc] [#ConfidentialFungibleToken-wit_PlaintextBalance]
#### [!toc] [#ConfidentialFungibleToken-wit_PlaintextBalance]
<APIItemCompact

  kind="witness"
  id="ConfidentialFungibleToken-wit_PlaintextBalance"
>
  Returns the wallet's cached plaintext for `ct`. The ciphertext is passed explicitly so the wallet can route by

#### wit_RandomnessSeed [toc] [#ConfidentialFungibleToken-wit_RandomnessSeed]
#### [!toc] [#ConfidentialFungibleToken-wit_RandomnessSeed]
<APIItemCompact
  circuitSig="wit_RandomnessSeed() → Bytes<32>"
  kind="witness"
  id="ConfidentialFungibleToken-wit_RandomnessSeed"
>
  Returns the 32-byte seed from which all per-operation randomness and every memo ephemeral is derived in-circuit.
  Not verified in-circuit.

  <Callout type='warn'>
    The seed must be fresh, high-entropy, and secret on every invocation. The derivation is deterministic and public,
    so anyone who knows a seed can read the amounts it masked, and reusing a seed across two operations leaks the
    difference between their amounts. On `transferFrom` and `_burnFrom` the seed is the spender's but the values it
    protects are the owner's.
  </Callout>
</APIItemCompact>

#### _mint [toc] [#ConfidentialFungibleToken-_mint]

<APIItemCompact
  circuitSig="_mint(account: Bytes<32>, value: Uint<128>) → []"
  kind="circuit"
  complexity="k=15, rows=27179"
  modulePrefix="ConfidentialFungibleToken"
>
  Credits `value` to `account`: homomorphically adds it to the account's pending ciphertext, pushes a memo carrying
  `value` encrypted to the recipient's key, and increments the recipient's credit counter. Performs no supply accounting;
  pair it with a supply tracker such as [_addSupply](#ConfidentialFungibleTokenPublicSupply-_addSupply).

  <Callout type='warn'>

    permissionless mint.
  </Callout>

  Requirements:

  * Contract is initialized.
  * `value` is at most `MAX_TRANSFER_VALUE()`.
  * `account` is not the zero account.
  * `account` is registered.
</APIItemCompact>

#### sweep [toc] [#ConfidentialFungibleToken-sweep]

  modulePrefix="ConfidentialFungibleToken"

#### approve [toc] [#ConfidentialFungibleToken-approve]
#### [!toc] [#ConfidentialFungibleToken-approve]
<APIItemCompact
  circuitSig="approve(spender: Bytes<32>, value: Uint<128>) → Bytes<32>"
  kind="circuit"
  complexity="k=16, rows=45114"
  modulePrefix="ConfidentialFungibleToken"
>
  Sets `spender`'s allowance over the caller's balance to `value` via an escrow: any prior escrow for `spender` is
  first refunded to the caller's spendable balance (a homomorphic add, no plaintext needed), then the caller's balance
  is debited by `value` and an [EscrowEntry](#ConfidentialFungibleToken-EscrowEntry) holding two encryptions of `value`
  plus an owner-readable memo of the remaining allowance is stored. Allowances replace rather than stack; `value` of
  zero revokes. Discloses the (owner, spender) pair, the owner's new balance ciphertext, and the entry; the cap is not
  disclosed.

  Returns the caller's (approver's) `accountId` for caller-side gating.

  Requirements:

  * Contract is initialized.
  * `value` is at most `MAX_TRANSFER_VALUE()`.
  * `spender` is not the zero account, is not the caller, and is registered.
  * The caller is registered, holds their encryption secret, and (after any prior escrow is refunded) has a spendable
    balance of at least `value`.
</APIItemCompact>

#### transferFrom [toc] [#ConfidentialFungibleToken-transferFrom]
#### [!toc] [#ConfidentialFungibleToken-transferFrom]
<APIItemCompact
  circuitSig="transferFrom(
    fromAddress: Bytes<32>,
    to: Bytes<32>,
    value: Uint<128>
  ) → Bytes<32>"
  kind="circuit"
  complexity="k=16, rows=72346"
  modulePrefix="ConfidentialFungibleToken"

>
  Spends `value` from the escrow that `fromAddress` granted the caller and credits it to `to`. The caller proves the

  Requirements:

  * Contract is initialized.
  * `value` is at most `MAX_TRANSFER_VALUE()`.
  * `to` is not the zero account and is registered.
  * An escrow from `fromAddress` to the caller exists, the caller holds their encryption secret, and the escrow holds
    at least `value`.
</APIItemCompact>

#### clearMemos [toc] [#ConfidentialFungibleToken-clearMemos]
#### [!toc] [#ConfidentialFungibleToken-clearMemos]
<APIItemCompact
  circuitSig="clearMemos(expectedEpoch: Uint<64>) → []"
  kind="circuit"
  complexity="k=13, rows=7053"
  modulePrefix="ConfidentialFungibleToken"
>
  Empties the caller's memo list. `expectedEpoch` is the caller's view of its `_creditEpochs` value when it read the
  list; the call is rejected if a credit has landed since, so a memo the caller has not seen is never discarded.

  <Callout type='warn'>
    Destructive. Spending requires proving the balance plaintext, which a wallet reconstructs from its memos or its
    cache. Clear only memos already folded into a durable, backed-up cache; otherwise the funds become unspendable.
  </Callout>

  Requirements:

  * Contract is initialized.
  * The caller is registered.
  * The caller holds the encryption secret registered for their account.
  * The caller has a memo list.
  * `expectedEpoch` equals the caller's current credit epoch.
</APIItemCompact>

## ConfidentialFungibleTokenPublicSupply [toc] [#ConfidentialFungibleTokenPublicSupply]
<APIGithubLinkHeader
    moduleName="ConfidentialFungibleTokenPublicSupply"
    link={`https://github.com/OpenZeppelin/compact-contracts/blob/v0.3.0/contracts/src/token/extensions/ConfidentialFungibleTokenPublicSupply.compact`}

```ts
import "./node_modules/@openzeppelin/compact-contracts/token/extensions/ConfidentialFungibleTokenPublicSupply";
```

Optional standalone extension that adds public supply accounting to a ConfidentialFungibleToken. It imports no token
module and needs no initialization. "Public" supply means the total, and therefore each mint and burn amount, is
disclosed on chain through the delta; balances stay confidential. The composing contract pairs each accounting block
with the matching token operation.
