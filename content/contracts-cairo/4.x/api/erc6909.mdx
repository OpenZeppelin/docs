  id="ERC6909Component-transfer"
  kind="external"
>
Transfers `amount` of token `id` from the caller to `receiver`.

Requirements:

- `receiver` is not the zero address.
- The caller has a balance of at least `amount` for token `id`.

Emits a [Transfer](#ERC6909Component-Transfer) event.
</APIItem>

<APIItem
  functionSignature="transfer_from(ref self: ContractState, sender: ContractAddress, receiver: ContractAddress, id: u256, amount: u256) → bool"
  id="ERC6909Component-transfer_from"
  kind="external"
>
Transfers `amount` of token `id` from `sender` to `receiver`. The caller's per-token allowance is spent unless the caller is `sender` or an operator for `sender`.

Requirements:

- `sender` and `receiver` are not the zero address.
- `sender` has a balance of at least `amount` for token `id`.
- The caller is `sender`, is an operator for `sender`, or has an allowance of at least `amount`.

Emits a [Transfer](#ERC6909Component-Transfer) event.
</APIItem>

<APIItem
  functionSignature="update(ref self: ContractState, sender: ContractAddress, receiver: ContractAddress, id: u256, amount: u256)"
  id="ERC6909Component-update"
  kind="internal"
>
Moves `amount` of token `id` from `sender` to `receiver`. A zero `sender` represents a mint, and a zero `receiver` represents a burn.

Invokes [before_update](#ERC6909Component-before_update) before balances change and [after_update](#ERC6909Component-after_update) after the `Transfer` event is emitted. Total supply tracking is provided by [ERC6909TokenSupplyComponent](#ERC6909TokenSupplyComponent).

Emits a [Transfer](#ERC6909Component-Transfer) event.
</APIItem>

<APIItem
  functionSignature="_set_operator(ref self: ContractState, owner: ContractAddress, spender: ContractAddress, approved: bool)"
  id="ERC6909Component-_set_operator"
  kind="internal"
>
Grants or revokes `spender`'s operator approval for `owner`.

Requirements:

- `owner` and `spender` are not the zero address.

Emits an [OperatorSet](#ERC6909Component-OperatorSet) event.
</APIItem>

<APIItem
  functionSignature="_spend_allowance(ref self: ContractState, owner: ContractAddress, spender: ContractAddress, id: u256, amount: u256)"
  id="ERC6909Component-_spend_allowance"
  kind="internal"
>
Reduces `spender`'s allowance for `owner` and token `id` by `amount`.

An allowance equal to the maximum `u256` value is treated as infinite and is not reduced. This function does not emit an `Approval` event.

Requirements:

- A finite allowance is at least `amount`.
</APIItem>

<APIItem
  functionSignature="_approve(ref self: ContractState, owner: ContractAddress, spender: ContractAddress, id: u256, amount: u256)"
  id="ERC6909Component-_approve"
  kind="internal"

Requirements:

- `owner` and `spender` are not the zero address.

Emits an [Approval](#ERC6909Component-Approval) event.
</APIItem>

<APIItem
  functionSignature="_transfer(ref self: ContractState, sender: ContractAddress, receiver: ContractAddress, id: u256, amount: u256)"
  id="ERC6909Component-_transfer"
  kind="internal"
>
Moves `amount` of token `id` from `sender` to `receiver` through [update](#ERC6909Component-update).

Requirements:

- `sender` and `receiver` are not the zero address.
- `sender` has a balance of at least `amount` for token `id`.

</APIItem>
